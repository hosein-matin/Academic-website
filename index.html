About

Analysis, computation, and control of nonlinear dynamical systems.

My research lies at the intersection of partial differential equations, dynamical systems, stochastic analysis, optimal control, and scientific machine learning. I am particularly interested in mathematically principled methods for nonlinear and nonsmooth dynamics, including conservation laws and coupled PDE–ODE systems.

Application areas include intelligent transportation systems, traffic-flow dynamics, crowd dynamics, and fluid dynamics. Before joining École Polytechnique in 2025, I was a Senior Researcher at the University of California, Berkeley.

Research

Core research directions

My work combines mathematical analysis with numerical and learning-based methods for complex dynamical systems.

01

Differential Equations

Analysis of nonlinear PDEs and ODEs, their coupling, well-posedness, stability, singular behavior, and conservation laws.

PDEsODEsConservation laws
02

Numerical & Learning-Based Solvers

Finite-volume and finite-element methods together with neural architectures designed to retain the structure of nonlinear PDEs.

Finite volumeNeural operatorsScientific ML
03

Optimal & Learning Control

Control and reinforcement-learning methods for dynamical systems, with emphasis on traffic-flow regulation and mixed-autonomy systems.

Optimal controlRLTraffic systems
