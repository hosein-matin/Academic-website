About

Research driven by structure, dynamics, and decisions.

My research develops analysis and computational methods for systems governed by differential equations. I am particularly interested in how PDE theory, optimal control, and modern learning methods can reinforce one another: analysis provides structure and guarantees, control provides decision-making principles, and learning provides flexible data-driven approximations.
